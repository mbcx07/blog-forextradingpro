Trading Framework

Developing a consistent trading approach requires understanding both technical and fundamental analysis. Technical analysis helps you identify entry and exit points, while fundamental analysis provides context for longer-term trends. The best traders combine both approaches.

Getting Started

  1. Learn the Basics: Understand pips, lots, leverage, and margin before trading real money.
  2. Use a Demo Account: Practice with virtual money until you're consistently profitable.
  3. Develop a Strategy: Create a trading plan with clear entry and exit rules.
  4. Manage Risk: Never risk more than 1-2% of your account on a single trade.
  5. Keep a Journal: Track every trade to learn from your successes and mistakes.

Conclusion

Forex trading is a skill that takes time to develop. Be patient, stay disciplined, and focus on consistent improvement rather than quick riches.
